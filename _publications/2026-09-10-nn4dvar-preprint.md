This preprint presents a machine-learning approach to 4D-Variational Data Assimilation in which a differentiable neural surrogate provides model sensitivities through automatic differentiation, eliminating the need for separately developed tangent-linear and adjoint models.

The manuscript is currently under review at the *Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems* (JAMES).
